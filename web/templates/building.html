If the heat fails

Call 311 (or file at portal.311.nyc.gov) and give your name and apartment number: since October 1, 2026, HPD must attempt an inspection for every non-anonymous heat complaint. From October 1 to May 31 landlords must keep apartments at 68°F by day when it is below 55°F outside, and 62°F overnight. This page describes the building's record; it is not an accusation.
