How it works

How to use HeatAhead
1

Detect

An hourly job on the LinuxONE instance reads the Open-Meteo 7-day forecast and looks for two or more freezing days in a row.

2

Decide

The model re-scores every building under the forecast temperatures (the whole city in about a third of a second on the s390x guest) and ranks them for inspection.

3

Respond

People who saved a building over the calibrated threshold get one email for that spell; inspectors get the ranked queue.
