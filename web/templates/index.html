How it works

1. Detect. An hourly job on the LinuxONE instance reads the Open-Meteo 7-day forecast and looks for two or more freezing days in a row.
2. Decide. The model re-scores every building under the forecast temperatures (the whole city in about a third of a second on the s390x guest) and ranks them for inspection.
3. Respond. Registered tenants and landlords over the calibrated threshold get one email for that spell; inspectors get the ranked queue.
