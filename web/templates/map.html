What am I seeing?

Color shows modeled winter risk. The citywide view groups properties into hexagons.

Zoom in to see mapped building shapes. Hexagons gradually fade into colored property markers; gray buildings provide context.

In the citywide 3D view, hexagon height shows complaint volume. Hexagons with zero complaints last season stay flat; try Flat map. Up close, building heights come from the map and may be estimated.

Click a hexagon to see its buildings, ordered by risk. Click an individual property marker for its record and aerial photo.
