Cookies & browser storage

We use an essential cookie for sign-in and security. Optional storage can remember your dashboard panel choices on this device. It stays off until you accept it. HeatAhead doesn't add analytics or advertising cookies.

ItemWhat it doesHow long
heatahead_accountEssential sign-in and security cookieUp to 30 days if you choose to stay signed in; otherwise the browser session, up to 12 hours
heatahead_privacyEssential browser storage that remembers your cookie choice180 days, unless you clear browser data
heatahead_panelsOptional local copy of your dashboard choicesUntil removed, or until the cookie choice expires or you reject optional storage

Rejecting optional storage keeps browsing and sign-in available. Settings you choose to save to your account are separate from this local copy.

Accounts & building alerts

Google sign-in supplies your verified email address, name and an account identifier. We don't receive your Google password or request Gmail, Drive or contacts access. Email-link sign-in uses your email address.

Saved building IDs, alert choices, dashboard preferences and any optional phone number you provide are stored on the team's server. You can remove saved buildings and stop building alerts from your account. We keep these while you use the service; contact us to request removal of remaining account information.

Public building records describe properties rather than individual tenants. Owner and agent details come from public registration records, with business addresses only.

Project-update emails

The newsletter asks for your email and an optional name. You join only after confirming the email link. It is separate from building heat alerts, and you don't need an account.

We store your signup, confirmation and subscription status on the team's server. The configured email provider processes the address and message to deliver confirmation emails and updates. Unsubscribe through the link in a newsletter; it doesn't change your building alerts. We retain subscription status to honor your choice. Contact us if you want your newsletter record removed.

We don't sell the newsletter list or automatically add people who sign in or save a building.

Maps, photos & other services

Map pages load map software from unpkg and map tiles from CARTO. Aerial photos load from Esri ArcGIS. Address search sends the address text to NYC Planning GeoSearch. Those services receive the normal network information needed to answer a request, such as your IP address.

Google Street View opens on Google's website when you choose its link. Google sign-in also takes you to Google. External services use their own privacy and cookie policies; HeatAhead's optional-storage control doesn't change those policies.

The site guide answers common questions locally. If AI matching is enabled, an unfamiliar question and the previous help topic can be sent to the configured provider to suggest a published help question. Account identity and earlier chat messages aren't included. The app doesn't save guide chats. Please keep personal details and secrets out of questions.

Vercel and the team's server process requests and operational logs to run the site. An email to our team is handled through Gmail. For privacy questions or a correction, contact {{ contact_email }}.
